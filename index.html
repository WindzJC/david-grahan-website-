BEHIND THE GRUMBLES

A little hello
from David.

David Graham is the children's author behind the Mr Grumblebum books. His illustrated adventures take readers to the fictional town of Farleigh, where ordinary days have a habit of turning into extraordinary escapades.

From a dash through the waxworks to a rescue by the lighthouse, these stories invite children and their grown-ups to share a few laughs and turn another page.
